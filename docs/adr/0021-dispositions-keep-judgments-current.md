# ADR 0021 – A hypothesis disposition keeps the judgments about it current, and an answered close states its open questions

Accepted with bn-2xmp; 2026-11-24. Refines plan §5.5 (freshness), §6.4, §8.6, §10.3 and §17. [Freshness](../../crates/investigation-core/src/freshness.rs) · [Closure](../../crates/investigation-core/src/case_ops.rs) · [Retrospective](../../crates/investigation-core/src/retrospective_ops.rs) · [Contract](../schema-v1.md#closing-a-case) · [Dogfood note](../../notes/dogfood/2026-11-24-perf08.md)

**Problem.** In the PERF.08 dogfood case, `case.close outcome=answered` committed while the case's only Question was open and its only Conclusion was effective `needs_review`. The analysis report then contradicted the closure. The Conclusion was `needs_review` because of its own transaction. That transaction recorded `hypothesis.disposition` for both hypotheses, citing the discriminating evidence. A disposition writes `evidence_refs` into the Hypothesis body. `evidence_refs` was not a lifecycle field of a Hypothesis, so revision 2 was a material revision. It made every judgment that cited revision 1 `needs_review`: the Assessments about the hypotheses (`subject_ref`) and about their rivals (`rival_expectations.rival_ref`), and through them the Conclusion. Ending the rejected rival raised a second trigger on `rival_ref`, because only `subject_ref` was treated as a judged record. A third problem came up at close. `question.resolve` could not share the closing transaction with `retrospective.record`, because the Question's new revision was accepted after the account's snapshot and so staled the account (`RETROSPECTIVE_STALE`).

**What the plan says.**

- Line 489: retraction, material correction, invalidated measurement, changed applicability or unresolved contradiction marks dependents `needs_review`. Line 491: lifecycle-only events do not invalidate, and the same distinction applies "to other freshness dependencies where display/lifecycle metadata is irrelevant".
- §10.3: currency is judged against the basis that a record read.
- §6.4, "Close substantive case": answer, choice or outcome, or an explicit inconclusive reason, plus outstanding questions, limitations and reopen triggers. Also §6.4: "Hard gates block *promotion*, never preservation of a surprising source or candid admission of uncertainty", and gates give machine-readable blockers and repair guidance. §6.6: "Closure is not a guarantee of correctness."
- §8.6: a material case closes with a Retrospective bound to a snapshot that "remains current for materially relevant work at closure". An updated account is needed only when "important new evidence or a changed decision basis intervened". The plan asks for "one atomic batch creating the retrospective and closing".
- The owner decision of 2026-11-24 keeps record-mode closure strict ([milestone B](../milestone-b.md#owner-decisions-2026-11-24)). This ADR does not change it.

**Decision.**

1. *Freshness rules version 8.* (a) A Hypothesis's `evidence_refs` is a lifecycle field. The evidence a disposition cites is its reason, recorded with `lifecycle` and `rationale`. It does not change the hypothesis's statement, applicability, falsifiers, question, assumptions or rivals. The edge stays `Inherit`, so a hypothesis still inherits freshness from that evidence when the evidence is withdrawn or corrected. (b) The rivals named by an Assessment's `rival_expectations.rival_ref` and by a confirmatory plan's `inquiry.confirmatory.predictions.rival_ref` are judged like a `subject_ref`. Ending such a rival, for example rejecting it within scope, does not make the judgment `needs_review`. A changed claim of the rival still does. The independent oracle in `investigation-store/tests/support` drops `evidence_refs` from a Hypothesis's substantive fields and classifies `rival_expectations.rival_ref` as a subject.
2. *An answered close states its open questions.* `case.close` with outcome `answered` is refused with `GATE_FAILED` at `/operations/N/payload/unresolved_questions` while a Question of the case has disposition `open` and is not listed in `unresolved_questions`. `error.expected` lists those Questions at their current revisions, in ID order. The message names `question.resolve` and gives the order. The check runs at the operation and again on the final batch state. Other outcomes are unchanged.
3. *A stale answer warns.* An answered close whose case has live Conclusions (not rejected, retracted or superseded), none of them effective `current`, commits with the warning `case.answer_not_current`. The warning names each Conclusion and the repair steps. This is a warning, not a refusal, because closure is not a promotion gate (§6.4, §6.6). The new blindspot check `closure.answered_without_current_conclusion` (Material) reports the same state on a closed case until its answer is current again.
4. *A resolution is not new work.* `check_retrospective` does not count a Question revision accepted after the snapshot when the Question existed at the snapshot with the same framing (all fields except `disposition`, `answer`, `resolution_reason` and `evidence_refs`) and every record the resolution cites was accepted at or before the snapshot. So `question.resolve`, `retrospective.record` and `case.close` may share one transaction, in that order. A new Question, a changed framing, or answer evidence recorded after the snapshot still stales the account.
5. *Documented order.* The `case.close` schema description, its catalog `when_to_use`, and the `retrospective` and `close` cards of the general pack give the order: resolve the questions, take the draft, record the account, close.

**Rejected alternatives.**

- *Exempt only the disposition's own transaction.* This needs transaction-aware freshness: the same revision would be material to one reader and not to another, depending on when the reader was accepted. It would also leave the same staling in place for a disposition recorded one call later.
- *Report the staled records in the disposition receipt.* The receipt would be correct and the case would still be stale. An agent that reads the receipt still has to re-assess, re-review and re-validate the Conclusion only because it recorded why a hypothesis was rejected.
- *Refuse an answered close when no Conclusion is current.* A refusal would block a candid close on a challenged answer. §6.4 limits hard gates to promotion. The warning and the blindspot check keep the disagreement visible.
- *Remove the open-question check for non-substantive cases.* An answered close with an open Question contradicts itself in every mode. The check costs one list entry or one `question.resolve`.

**Consequences.**

- *Compatibility.* No schema shape change, no new error code and no new enum value. The `case.close` schema gains descriptions only (ADR 0007). `FRESHNESS_RULES_VERSION` is 8. Views that report it change, and so does the golden at `fixtures/development/brief-show-json.json`. An answered close that was accepted before can now be refused. Scenario A's proof now lists the reviewer's open scheduler question as unresolved.
- *Check set.* The new check changes `check_set_digest`. A blindspot Review recorded under the earlier check set becomes `needs_review` (`check_set_changed`), as designed. A journal that replays such a review through the commit path is refused, so the Scenario B fixture workspace was rebuilt from its proof. Its vault golden, the walkthrough B IDs and the report tests' IDs changed with it.
- *Fewer re-reviews.* Disposing of hypotheses no longer forces re-assessment of the judgments that told them apart. A Conclusion that names a rejected hypothesis as a causal factor (`causal_factors.hypothesis_ref`, an inherited basis) is still `needs_review` when that hypothesis ends.

**Tests.** Each fails without its fix. In `freshness::tests`: `disposing_of_hypotheses_on_an_assessment_keeps_the_judgments_current` (rule 1, including a control that a changed claim still stales the judgment) and `an_answered_close_on_a_stale_answer_warns_and_the_audit_reports_it` (rule 3 and the blindspot check, with a current control). In `retrospective_ops::tests`: `an_answered_close_resolves_or_lists_every_open_question` (rule 2) and `resolving_a_question_in_the_closing_batch_keeps_the_account_current` (rule 4, with a control that a new Question still stales the account). Scenario A's proof asserts the refusal and its `expected` list.
