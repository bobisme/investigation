# ADR 0017 – A supporting validation review answers the open objections on its subject

Accepted with bn-4job; 2026-11-24. Refines plan §6.4, §10.3 and §14.3. [Rule](../../crates/investigation-core/src/review_ops.rs) · [Contract](../schema-v1.md#validation-reviews-over-open-objections) · [Rehearsal](../../scripts/trial-tasks.py)

**Problem.** The W14.03 rehearsal found that a reader who never saw a challenge can record a supporting validation review over it. In task 5 (stale resume), a successor builds a review from the basis an earlier handoff brief showed. After the brief, the predecessor raised a material objection on the conclusion. The objection does not change the conclusion's revision, basis digest or required read set, so the stale review was accepted. In task 7 stage 2, a reader that sees only summaries fills the basis digest and read set from the refusals' `expected` values. Nothing in that loop shows it an objection. In both cases the core then computes the review's membership sets (§10.3) at acceptance, so the stored review says that its author judged an open-objection set that includes a challenge the author never read. The objection then no longer counts as raised after the review, which is the rule that makes a reviewed subject `disputed` (§10.3).

**What the plan says.** It does not state this rule. §6.4 and §14.3 say that an unresolved critical objection blocks Conclusion validation, and §14.3 and §20.1 say that a material objection must stay visible and cannot be suppressed. §10.3 says that a review's basis includes set membership, such as "no open objections". §9.4 gives `review.submit` an `objection_refs` field ("reviewer method, evidence seen, source dependencies, verdict, gaps and objections"), and the core already requires each cited objection to concern the subject and to be in the read set. None of these says what happens when a supporting review leaves out an open objection.

**Decision.** A `review.submit` with `kind: validation` and `verdict: supports` is refused while an open Objection names its subject and the review does not cite it in `objection_refs`.

1. *Scope.* The rule covers every open objection whose `subject_ref` is the reviewed Conclusion, Decision or Comparison, at any revision. It covers both severities: a critical objection is a stronger challenge than a material one. It reads the state at the operation's place in the batch, so an objection raised earlier in the same transaction counts. Objections on other records, including records of the subject's dependency closure, are not covered: a review of the subject cannot cite them (`objection_refs` must concern the subject), and the existing promotion checks and effective-status rules already see them.
2. *Answering.* Citing an objection is the explicit, documented way for a review to address it (§9.4). The cited revision must be current and in the read set, as before. The reviewer can also resolve or withdraw the objection first (§14.3), or submit `objects` or `inconclusive`. Those verdicts and blindspot reviews are not refused.
3. *Error.* `GATE_FAILED` (exit 5), the code that every other promotion and review-basis refusal uses, at `/operations/N/payload/objection_refs`. `error.actual.open_objections` lists each unanswered objection as `{objection_ref, subject_ref, severity}` in ID order, the shape that `challenges.open_objections` in `show` uses. It does not include the statement: objection text is read from the objection, under its own classification. `repair.argv` is `show OBJECTION --depth full` for the first one ([ADR 0018](0018-review-refusals-name-the-read.md) changed it to `--depth summary`, which carries the statement). The error gives no `expected` value. A reader that copies values from repairs must read the objection to answer it, or make an explicit claim that it did.
4. *Validation is unchanged.* `conclusion.validate` still refuses an open critical objection and only warns about a material one. A review that cites a material objection can still validate the conclusion, and the objection stays open and visible. An objection raised after the review still makes the subject `disputed` (§10.3 membership).

**Rejected alternatives.**

- *Refuse only critical objections, or none.* A critical objection already blocks validation, but the stale review would still be recorded as having judged it. A material one would still leave the task-5 shape open.
- *Record the review and mark it `needs_review`.* This needs a new freshness trigger. Under ADR 0007 that is a change to a frozen output enum, and the stale review would still be in the journal as a supporting review of a disputed claim.
- *Refuse the later `conclusion.validate` instead.* This leaves the misleading review in the journal and changes the rule that material objections do not block validation (§14.3).
- *Put the open objections in `expected`.* A reader that copies repair values would answer the objection without reading it. The rehearsal's summary-only reader does exactly that with `expected`.

**Consequences.**

- *Compatibility.* No schema changes, no new error code and no new enum value. A request that was accepted before can now be refused with `GATE_FAILED`. Callers that submit supporting reviews must cite open objections on the subject. Five test helpers and the freshness oracle's generator now do this.
- *The citation is a claim, not proof.* The core cannot check that a reviewer read what it cites. The task-5 rehearsal shows that a successor who copies the objection references from the refusal into the review still commits, and that the task grader still fails it. This is the same limit as a self-reported `human_confirmation` (§16.1).
- *Blinded review.* A review packet lists objections to the subject only by reference, in `omitted_records`. A blinded reviewer can form a judgment first and then read the objections before submitting a supporting verdict, or submit `inconclusive`.
- *Same batch.* A reviewer who raises an objection with the review as its originating `review_ref` puts the review first in the batch. That keeps working. A batch that raises an objection and then submits an uncited supporting review is refused. The objection's exact revision is not known before the batch, so that review cannot cite it in its read set.

**Tests.** `review_ops::tests` (core): `a_supporting_review_from_a_stale_snapshot_must_answer_a_later_objection` (task 5), `a_review_built_from_repair_hints_cannot_skip_an_open_objection` (task 7 stage 2, material and critical, with a control that commits) and `batch_order_and_objections_on_other_records`. All three fail without the check. `objections.rs` (CLI) checks the error's path, objection list and repair, and that a disputing review commits. `scripts/trial-tasks.py rehearse`: the task-5 stale review is refused and grades as a pass, and a review that copies the handed objection reference commits and grades as a fail. A task-7 stage-2 guardrails-off run with a material objection is refused at the review.
