When did the Mill Lane footbridge open?
Parish newsletter, 1988: "the footbridge, opened in 1931".
Walking guide, 2004: "opened 1931", citing the 1960 town history.
Heritage leaflet, 2011: "opened 1931", citing the 1960 town history.
Town history, 1960 edition: "the footbridge was opened in 1931".
Museum catalogue entry 44/7: an opening plaque for the footbridge, dated 1933.
