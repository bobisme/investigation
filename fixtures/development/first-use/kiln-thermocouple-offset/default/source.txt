Studio kiln K2: reading versus witness cones
Ceramics studio maintenance notes

Kiln controller: type K thermocouple, installed three years ago, never replaced or recalibrated.
Firing 112 (glaze firing, target cone 6)

Controller peak reading: 1204 C
Witness cones on the middle shelf, centre position:
  cone 5 fully bent, cone 6 fully bent, cone 7 tip touching the shelf
Published cone chart for this heating rate: cone 6 bends at about 1222 C, cone 7 at about 1240 C.

Reading of the cones: the ware reached at least the cone 6 temperature, about 1222 C, so the controller read about 18 C low or more.

Context
- Type K thermocouples commonly drift as they age at glaze temperatures.
- The studio had two underfired-looking glaze loads this term, which the controller showed as reaching target.

Limits
- Only firing 112 had witness cones. One firing.
- Cones were on one shelf position only; the kiln is known to run cooler near the door, and no cones were placed there.
- No reference thermocouple was used.
