Tram door sensor gateway: transport choice
Rolling stock engineering note

Hard requirement
T1. No door-event message may be lost during a tunnel radio dropout of up to 30 seconds.

Preferences
T2. Lower end-to-end latency in normal service.
T3. Use equipment the depot already maintains.

Option X: direct publish over the radio link
- Each sensor message is sent immediately. During a dropout messages are discarded.
- Bench test: 30-second dropout, 1,800 messages sent, 1,212 received.
- Median latency in normal service: 0.4 s.

Option Y: gateway buffer with store-and-forward
- The on-board gateway writes messages to flash and forwards them after the link returns.
- Bench test: 30-second dropout at 200 messages per second, 6,000 messages sent, 6,000 received in order.
- Median latency in normal service: 0.9 s.
- The gateway model is already maintained at the depot.

Test conditions
- Peak traffic measured on line 3 is 600 messages per second at the morning door cycle.
- Option Y has only been tested at 200 messages per second, one third of peak. Flash write speed at peak was not tested.
