Claim check: "the river reached a record 6.1 m"

Item 1: parish newsletter, March issue
"Last month the river reached a record 6.1 metres at Millford bridge, the highest ever measured there."

Item 2: data extract from the regional water office, station Millford Bridge
Station history
  1978 to 2021: gauge on the north pier
  2022: gauge relocated 200 m downstream to the weir footbridge; readings at the new site are not comparable with the old site without a datum adjustment, which has not yet been published.
Annual maxima (stage, metres)
  2019  5.6  (old site)
  2020  5.9  (old site)
  2021  5.2  (old site)
  2022  4.8  (new site)
  2023  5.5  (new site)
  2024  6.1  (new site, provisional; data not yet quality checked)

Item 3: note from the water office help desk
"Provisional values can change after quality checks. We do not describe a reading as a record until the series is adjusted for the 2022 relocation."
