Ticket storage options for the harbour ferry kiosks
Prepared for the operations group

Hard requirement
R1. A kiosk must keep selling and validating tickets for a whole shift with the dock network down. Dock network outages have lasted up to 9 hours (operations log, last two winters).

Preferences
P1. Lower monthly cost.
P2. Fewer moving parts for the two-person IT team.

Option A: hosted ticket service
- Every sale and validation is a call to the hosted service.
- Vendor documentation: "Kiosks require connectivity. No offline mode is offered."
- Cost: 310 per month.

Option B: kiosk-local store with nightly upload
- Each kiosk writes sales to a local database and uploads them when the network returns.
- Offline trial: kiosk K3 sold and validated 212 tickets during a planned 2-hour disconnection on 3 February. Upload after reconnection completed with no missing sales.
- No trial has run longer than 2 hours.
- Cost: 420 per month including the upload service.

Open items
- Local storage on the kiosks is 8 GB; expected use for a 9-hour shift was not measured.
