Community choir: how to share rehearsal scores
Committee working paper, autumn term

Hard requirement
H1. Every singer must be able to get the current score without creating any online account. Several members have said they will not create accounts.

Soft preferences
S1. Scores update within a day of the director's changes.
S2. Low volunteer effort.

Option 1: shared cloud folder
- Members open the folder after signing in with a personal account on the storage service.
- Updates appear immediately.
- Volunteer effort: low.

Option 2: printed binder at rehearsals plus a public link to the PDF
- The librarian prints changed pages each Thursday; the PDF link works without signing in.
- Updates reach the binder within a week; the link is updated the same day.
- Volunteer effort: about one hour a week.

Option 3: email the PDF to the member list
- Needs an email address, not an account on any service.
- Two members have no email address.

Member survey
- 11 of 40 members replied. 4 of the 11 said they would not create an account.
- The other 29 members did not reply, so the number unwilling or unable to use accounts is not known.
